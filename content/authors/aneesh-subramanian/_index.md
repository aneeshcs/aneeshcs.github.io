Aneesh C. Subramanian is an Associate Professor in the [Department of Atmospheric and Oceanic Sciences (ATOC)](https://www.colorado.edu/atoc/) at the University of Colorado Boulder. He is also a visiting scientist at the [Center for Western Weather and Water Extremes (CW3E)](https://cw3e.ucsd.edu/) at Scripps Institution of Oceanography, UC San Diego, and an international collaborator in the Geophysical Flows Lab at the Indian Institute of Technology Madras.

His research group — the **Climate Processes and Predictability Group** — works on processes that influence extreme weather and climate variability across multiple timescales, with a focus on developing next-generation forecasting tools that combine physics-based modeling with machine learning and data assimilation.

## Research Interests

- Predictability and dynamics of weather and climate
- Subseasonal-to-seasonal (S2S) prediction
- Atmospheric river dynamics and forecasting
- Tropical climate and weather dynamics, including the Madden-Julian Oscillation (MJO)
- Tropical–extratropical teleconnections
- Machine learning for prediction, parameterization, and data assimilation
- Stochastic parameterization in Earth system models
- Data assimilation in coupled ocean–atmosphere models
- Multi-scale Earth system modeling and high-performance computing

## Background

Prior to joining CU Boulder, Aneesh held a visiting scholar and postdoctoral position in the Predictability of Weather and Climate group in the Physics Department at the University of Oxford.

He received his Ph.D. (2012) from the Climate, Atmosphere, Seas, and Paleo-Environments (CASPO) program at Scripps Institution of Oceanography, UC San Diego, where he worked with Prof. Arthur J. Miller and Prof. Bruce Cornuelle on multiscale dynamics of atmospheric and oceanic variability, data assimilation in ocean modeling, and air–sea interaction in the Asian Summer Monsoon region.

He holds an M.Sc. (Engineering) from the Center for Atmospheric and Oceanic Sciences at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore, and a B.Tech from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Madras.

## Selected Research Projects

**Atmospheric River Forecasting** — Combining statistical and dynamical approaches to improve probabilistic forecasts of integrated vapor transport (IVT) and atmospheric rivers affecting the U.S. West Coast, including development of operational AI-based ensemble forecasting systems.

**Subseasonal Predictability** — Studying sources of predictability at the 2–6 week range, with emphasis on the MJO and its influence on extreme precipitation, heat waves, and atmospheric rivers.

**Machine Learning for Earth System Modeling** — Developing neural network emulators, diffusion-based ensemble generators, and graph transformer architectures for climate projection and weather forecasting.

**Data Assimilation** — Advancing ensemble Kalman filter and adjoint methods for coupled ocean–atmosphere systems, incorporating ship and satellite observations.

**Greenland Ice Sheet Dynamics** — Investigating surface melt processes using variable-resolution climate models and causal discovery methods to attribute melt to specific atmospheric drivers.
