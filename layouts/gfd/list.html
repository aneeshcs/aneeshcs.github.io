Geophysical Fluid Dynamics

An interactive graduate textbook on rotating, stratified geophysical flows: long-form text alongside a runnable model for every core concept. Each chapter executes entirely in your browser via WebAssembly — no installation required.

The notebooks that used to live on this page have grown into a full textbook — 24 chapters across seven parts, built on a shared, tested numerical library.

Read the textbook →
  • I · Foundations — kinematics, governing equations, rotation, scaling
  • II · Balance & adjustment — geostrophy, shallow water, vorticity & PV
  • III · Quasi-geostrophy — the QG approximation, Rossby waves, two-layer modes
  • IV · Stratified flow & waves — vertical modes, internal gravity waves, wave–mean interaction
  • V · Instabilities — convection to Lorenz, barotropic, baroclinic, symmetric & KH
  • VI · Turbulence & circulation — geostrophic turbulence, eddy transport, wind-driven gyres, overturning
  • VII · Structure (optional capstone) — Hamiltonian GFD, wave activity, balanced models

The four original explorers — Rayleigh–Bénard convection, geostrophic adjustment, 2D turbulence and internal gravity waves — are now chapters 14, 6, 18 and 12.

Built with marimo & NumPy · Runs in-browser via Pyodide WebAssembly · Source on GitHub
